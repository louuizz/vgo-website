The calm way to cross the Causeway.

How much is a private car from Singapore to Johor Bahru?

Price depends on the vehicle, pick-up and drop-off points, time of day and whether it is one-way or return. V-GO gives a fixed all-in quote before you confirm, so you know the total with no meter and no surprise surcharges. Message us on WhatsApp for an instant quote.

Can I stay inside the car at the Singapore–Johor checkpoints?

For most private transfers passengers remain seated in the vehicle while the driver handles the process, though officers may occasionally ask passengers to alight or show documents. Always carry a valid passport and complete the required arrival cards (MDAC for Malaysia and SG Arrival Card for Singapore).

How long does the trip between JB and Singapore take?

Without heavy queues, it is usually about one to two hours door to door. Peak periods such as Friday evenings, weekends, school holidays and public holidays can take longer, so we recommend leaving early and we will advise the best pick-up time.

What vehicles do you use for private MPV charter?

Our core fleet is the Toyota Alphard and Vellfire premium MPV, seating up to 6 passengers with luggage space. For larger groups we can arrange a second vehicle or a larger van on request.

Do you offer day charters with a driver in Johor?

Yes. You can hire a chauffeured Alphard for a full day in Johor Bahru, Legoland, Desaru, Forest City or beyond. We plan the itinerary around your stops for shopping, food, business or sightseeing and agree a fixed day rate in advance.
