Choosing your checkpoint

Your driver chooses between the Woodlands (Causeway) and Tuas (Second Link) checkpoints based on conditions at that hour and your destination, so you take the quicker crossing.

Best times to travel

Weekday mornings are generally smoother. Friday evenings, weekends, school holidays and public holidays are the busiest. Tell us your time and we advise the best pick-up.
