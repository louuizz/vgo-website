Private transfer from Changi Airport to West Malaysia

Collect your bags, walk out and your chauffeur is waiting. One vehicle all the way from Changi to your hotel, home or office in Johor Bahru, Melaka, Kuala Lumpur or beyond.

Get a fixed quoteChat on WhatsApp

How it works

Send your flight number and passenger and luggage count. We confirm the vehicle and a fixed price, then track your flight. Your chauffeur meets you on arrival, helps with your bags and drives you through the checkpoint to your destination in Malaysia.

Why not a taxi or shared bus

  • No changing vehicles at the border
  • No queueing for a ride after a long flight
  • A fixed price instead of a meter
  • Room for the whole family and all the luggage

Before you land

  • Complete the SG Arrival Card for Singapore before arrival if you enter Singapore
  • Have your passport ready for the land checkpoint
  • Check the Malaysia entry requirements for your nationality

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How long is the drive from Changi to Johor Bahru?

Typically one and a half to two and a half hours including the checkpoint, depending on traffic and your destination.

What if my flight lands late at night?

We operate around the clock and track your flight, so late arrivals are no problem. Let us know when you book.

Can the driver help with luggage?

Yes. Tell us your luggage count in advance so we confirm that the vehicle fits everything comfortably.
