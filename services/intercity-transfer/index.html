Intercity private MPV trips across West Malaysia

Comfortable seating, room for luggage and a driver who handles the road. Travel between Kuala Lumpur, Melaka, Ipoh, Penang and the highlands at your own pace.

Indicative drive times from Kuala Lumpur

  • Genting Highlands: about 1 to 1.5 hours
  • Melaka: about 2 to 2.5 hours
  • Ipoh: about 2 to 2.5 hours
  • Cameron Highlands: about 3 to 3.5 hours
  • Penang: about 4 to 5 hours

Why go private

A private MPV removes bus timetables and transfers. You leave when you want, stop when you need to and arrive with your group and luggage together. It suits families, friends travelling together and business travellers who want to work or rest on the way.

Planning an intercity trip

  • Tell us your destination, date and group size for a fixed quote
  • Rest and meal stops can be planned along the route
  • One-way and return trips are both available
  • Overnight stays by agreement

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can I book a one-way trip from Kuala Lumpur to Penang?

Yes. One-way and return trips are both available. We confirm the all-in price before you commit.

Will the driver wait for a same-day return?

Yes, a same-day return can be arranged. Tell us the waiting time you need and we include it in the quote.

How is the price for intercity trips calculated?

It depends on the route, the vehicle, whether the trip is one-way or return and any waiting time. You receive one fixed all-in price in advance.
