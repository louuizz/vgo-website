About V-GO

We make travel between Singapore and West Malaysia calm, predictable and comfortable.

Who we are

V-GO is a premium private transport service for travellers, families, executives and tour groups moving between Singapore and West Malaysia. We replace the taxi queue, the shared van and the surprise surcharge with a chauffeured MPV that is yours alone for the whole journey.

Our name says what we do: you tell us where, and we go. Our tagline says how: your journey begins here, at your door.

What we stand for

  • Fixed, all-in pricing confirmed before you travel
  • Professional, courteous chauffeurs
  • Clean, comfortable, well-maintained vehicles
  • Clear communication on WhatsApp, around the clock
  • Honest advice about timing, documents and checkpoint conditions

Where we operate

We serve Singapore, including Changi Airport, and all of West Malaysia: Johor Bahru, Melaka, Kuala Lumpur and the Klang Valley, Genting Highlands, Ipoh, Cameron Highlands and Penang, plus KLIA, KLIA2 and Senai airports. Tell us your destination and we confirm a fixed price.

Licensing and safety

Cross-border passenger transport must be properly licensed and insured in both countries. Ask us for our licence details at any time, and we encourage you to ask any operator the same question before you book.
