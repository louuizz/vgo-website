Frequently asked questions

Everything travellers ask before booking a private transfer or charter between Singapore and Johor Bahru.

How much is a private car from Singapore to Johor Bahru?

Price depends on the vehicle, pick-up and drop-off points, time of day and whether it is one-way or return. V-GO gives a fixed all-in quote before you confirm, so you know the total with no meter and no surprise surcharges. Message us on WhatsApp for an instant quote.

Can I stay inside the car at the Singapore–Johor checkpoints?

For most private transfers passengers remain seated in the vehicle while the driver handles the process, though officers may occasionally ask passengers to alight or show documents. Always carry a valid passport and complete the required arrival cards (MDAC for Malaysia and SG Arrival Card for Singapore).

How long does the trip between JB and Singapore take?

Without heavy queues, it is usually about one to two hours door to door. Peak periods such as Friday evenings, weekends, school holidays and public holidays can take longer, so we recommend leaving early and we will advise the best pick-up time.

What vehicles do you use for private MPV charter?

Our core fleet is the Toyota Alphard and Vellfire premium MPV, seating up to 6 passengers with luggage space. For larger groups we can arrange a second vehicle or a larger van on request.

Do you offer day charters with a driver in Johor?

Yes. You can hire a chauffeured Alphard for a full day in Johor Bahru, Legoland, Desaru, Forest City or beyond. We plan the itinerary around your stops for shopping, food, business or sightseeing and agree a fixed day rate in advance.

Can you pick me up at Changi Airport or Senai Airport?

Yes. We offer meet-and-greet airport transfers at Changi and Senai with flight tracking, so your chauffeur adjusts to delays. Send your flight number when you book.

Is V-GO licensed and insured for cross-border transfers?

Cross-border passenger services must hold the correct Malaysian and Singapore licences and passenger insurance. V-GO operates with licensed vehicles and insured journeys, and you can request our licence details before booking. We recommend asking any operator for the same.

How do I pay and what is the cancellation policy?

We accept payment by bank transfer, PayNow, cash (SGD or MYR) and other methods confirmed at booking. Free changes and cancellation are available up to a set time before pick-up — the exact window is stated in your quote.

Can I book a one-way trip, a return, or multiple stops?

All three. One-way and return transfers are standard, and additional stops can be added within the route for a small, pre-agreed charge. Tell us your plan and we will price it in one quote.

How far in advance should I book?

We recommend 24–48 hours ahead, and earlier for weekends, public holidays and school breaks. Last-minute requests are welcome — message us and we will confirm availability quickly.

Which areas do you serve?

Our core corridor is Johor Bahru and Singapore, including Changi and Senai airports. We also arrange private trips anywhere in West Malaysia, such as Melaka, Kuala Lumpur, Ipoh and Penang. Tell us your destination and we quote it.

Do you provide child seats?

Child seats are available on request and subject to availability. Tell us the ages of the children when you book.

How many suitcases fit in the vehicle?

Our Alphard comfortably takes up to four large suitcases depending on the seating layout. Tell us your luggage count and we confirm the right vehicle.
