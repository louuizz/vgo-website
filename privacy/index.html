Privacy policy

How we handle the information you share with us.

Information we collect

When you request a quote we receive the details you choose to send, such as your name, trip details, flight number and contact number. The booking form on this website does not store anything: it prepares a WhatsApp message that you send yourself.

How we use it

We use your details only to quote, confirm and operate your trip and to contact you about it. We do not sell your personal information.

Sharing

We share trip details with the driver who carries out your booking. We may share information when the law requires it or when needed to operate the border crossing.

Retention and your rights

We keep booking records only as long as needed for service, accounting and legal reasons. You may ask us to access, correct or delete your information by contacting us.

Third-party services

This website loads fonts from a third-party provider and links to WhatsApp. Those services have their own privacy practices.
